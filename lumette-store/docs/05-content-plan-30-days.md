# 5. 30-day organic content plan (TikTok, Reels, Pinterest)

**Goal of the 30 days:** find which 2-3 products and angles get saves, shares, add-to-carts and orders, so you know what deserves Google Ads budget. Not "go viral". No result is guaranteed; treat every number below as a starting heuristic.

## Ground rules
- Film your own footage with products you've received (order samples in week 0). Don't re-post other creators' videos without written permission.
- No fake before/afters, no invented testimonials, no "as seen on". If a creator or friend gets a free product, disclose it (#gifted / #ad, per FTC and Canadian Competition Bureau guidance).
- No health or anti-aging claims. Describe what the tool *is* and what you *do* with it.
- Always show the real product at the start (the 3-second "what is this?" rule), vertical 9:16, text on screen, captions on (accessibility + most views are muted).
- Delivery honesty: if a comment asks, answer with the real window.
- Cross-post the same video to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and a Pinterest Idea/Video Pin. Remove the TikTok watermark for cross-posting (use the original export).

About 12-14 videos in 30 days; 10 distinct ideas below (some reshot with different products).

## Week-by-week
- **Week 0 (before day 1):** receive samples, shoot 3-4 hours of b-roll (each product: unboxing, in use, close-ups, scale, sound of use), set up profiles (bio: what you sell + delivery honesty + link), 9 Pinterest boards (one per collection + "Routines").
- **Week 1 (days 1-7): Establish.** Videos #1 (unboxing), #2 (heatless curls overnight), #3 (organizer reveal). Post at consistent times; measure 3-second hold and average watch time.
- **Week 2 (days 8-14): Double down.** Reshoot the best format with a different product. Videos #4, #5, #6. Start tracking saves/shares per video.
- **Week 3 (days 15-21): Educate.** Videos #7, #8, #9 (comparisons/how-to). Add a "link in bio/product tag" CTA to every video. Start collecting real reviews via the reviews app post-delivery email.
- **Week 4 (days 22-30): Decide.** Video #10 plus the best 2 re-cuts. Review data; pick the product(s) that pass the Ads gate (docs/03). Write up what you learned.

## Pinterest (3 pins/day)
- Pin each product image + each video, 3 sizes (2:3 vertical): clean product, in-use, flat-lay.
- Titles carry the keyword ("Heatless curling ribbon set - overnight waves without heat"); description 2 sentences + 2-3 keywords; destination = the product page; board per collection.
- Pinterest content has a long tail; many pins get most traffic weeks after posting. Don't judge it at day 30.

## What to measure (weekly)
| Metric | Where | Target to continue |
|---|---|---|
| 3-sec hold, avg watch time | TikTok/Reels analytics | > 35-40% hold (rule of thumb) |
| Saves + shares per 1,000 views | Platform | > 10 saves per 1,000 |
| Link clicks, sessions from social | GA4 acquisition | trend up week over week |
| Product views -> add to cart | GA4 | > 5% |
| Orders from organic | Shopify Analytics | first 5 = signal |
| Email signups | Shopify customers | > 2-3% of sessions |

## Decision at day 30
1. Rank products by organic orders and add-to-cart rate, then by margin before ads.
2. Products that pass the gate in docs/03 become the first ad products. The rest stay organic.
3. Re-shoot the best angle with new product variants; retire formats that failed twice.
