This policy explains how and when {{BRAND}} ({{LEGAL_NAME}}) ships orders to customers in the United States and Canada.

How fulfilment works

We do not keep stock in a warehouse of our own. When you place an order, it is passed to our fulfilment partner, which packs it and hands it to a carrier. Depending on the product, your parcel may ship from a warehouse in China, the United States or Canada. The product page tells you which, and shows an estimated delivery window.

Processing time

Orders are normally processed within 1-3 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. Processing can take longer during sales periods and holidays.

Estimated delivery times

These are estimates counted from the day you place your order (processing plus transit). They are not guarantees. Carriers can run late, and customs can add time.

Ships fromTypical delivery
Warehouse in China8-18 business days
Warehouse in the United States3-8 business days
Warehouse in Canada3-9 business days

If an order contains items from different warehouses, they may arrive separately and you will receive a tracking number for each parcel.

Shipping rates

Shipping is free on orders of USD $50 or more (CAD $65 or more for Canadian orders), calculated on the order subtotal after discounts. Below that, a flat rate shown at checkout applies. Rates are always displayed before you pay.

Tracking

We email you a tracking number as soon as one is available. It can take 2-5 days after dispatch for the carrier's system to show movement. You can follow your parcel on our Track your order page.

Duties, taxes and customs

Sales tax is calculated at checkout where it applies. Parcels crossing a border may be subject to import duties, customs fees or brokerage charges. Where the carrier gives us those amounts in advance they are shown at checkout. Any amount that is instead collected on delivery is the recipient's responsibility. Refusing a parcel because of such charges does not entitle you to a refund of shipping costs.

Delivery address

Please check your address carefully at checkout. We cannot redirect an order after it has shipped. If a parcel is returned to sender because of an incorrect or incomplete address, we can reship it once you cover the new shipping cost.

Lost, delayed or damaged parcels

If your tracking has not moved for 10 business days, or your order has not arrived 10 business days after the end of its estimated window, contact us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with your order number. We will investigate with the carrier and either resend the order or refund you. If an order arrives damaged, see our Returns and Refunds policy.

Where we ship

We currently ship to the United States and Canada only. We do not ship to PO boxes where a carrier requires a street address.
