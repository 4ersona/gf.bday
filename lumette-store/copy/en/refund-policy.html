We want you to be happy with your purchase. This policy explains your options if you are not.

30-day returns

You may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the item price, if they are unused, in their original condition and in their original packaging. To start a return, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with your order number and the item(s) you want to return. Please do not send anything back before we reply, because we need to give you the correct return instructions.

Return shipping

If you are returning an item because you changed your mind, you are responsible for return shipping costs, and original shipping fees are not refunded. If the item is damaged, defective or not what you ordered, we cover the cost (see below).

Damaged, defective or incorrect items

If your item arrives damaged, does not work, or is not what you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery. Send your order number and clear photos of the item and the packaging. We will send a replacement or give you a full refund, including shipping, whichever you prefer. In many cases we will not ask you to return the item.

Items we cannot accept back

For hygiene reasons we cannot accept returns of opened or used items that touch skin, eyes or hair, including makeup brushes, sponges, lash tools, face rollers and hair accessories, unless they are defective or incorrect. Gift cards are not refundable.

Refunds

When your return is approved (or when we approve a refund without a return), we refund the original payment method within 5-10 business days. Your bank or card issuer may take additional time to show the credit. If you do not see a refund after that, check with your bank first, then contact us.

Late or missing refunds

Check your bank account again, then contact your card company, then email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will look into it.

Exchanges

If you want a different size or colour, the quickest way is to place a new order for the item you want and return the original under this policy.

Cancellations

Orders are sent to our fulfilment partner quickly. We can cancel an order only if it has not yet been dispatched, usually within about 12 hours of ordering. Contact us as soon as possible.

Return address

Do not return items to the address on the shipping label. Use only the return address we give you in writing: {{RETURN_ADDRESS}}.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under the consumer protection laws that apply where you live.
